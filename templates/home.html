What people ask first

Do I need to share my Aadhaar number?

No. Arhata never asks for Aadhaar, PAN, bank or ration card numbers. If you type one by mistake, we remove it before reading your message. You show your Aadhaar only on the official portal.

Does “likely eligible” mean I will get it?

No. It means your details meet every rule we know of. The scheme authority makes the final decision after checking your documents.

Is this a government website?

No. It is a free guide that points you to the official portals: Telangana ePASS, the National Scholarship Portal, AICTE and PM-Vidyalaxmi.

What if the rules change?

Every scheme's rules are stored as data with the date they were last checked. When a government order changes a limit, we update one number and every result follows.

Someone is charging me to apply. Should I pay?

Never pay anyone to apply for a government scholarship. The application is free. A Mee Seva centre charges only the official certificate fee.
