Why Arhata exists

India has hundreds of scholarships for students like you. Many eligible families never claim them: the rules are hard to read, the document list is unclear, and middlemen charge for help that should be free.

How it works

Tell

Describe yourself in plain words, typed or spoken, in English, Telugu or Hindi. We only ask a quick question when a missing detail would change a result.

See why

A plain rules engine checks every scheme. Each result shows the rule it rests on and your answer. AI only helps read your message; it never decides.

Apply and track

Your document checklist, the steps on the official portal, and reminders before the last date, saved on your own phone.

Our privacy promise

  • {{ icon('shield-check', 22) }}We never ask for Aadhaar, PAN, bank, ration card or any ID number. If one is typed by mistake, it is removed before anything reads the message.
  • {{ icon('check-square', 22) }}Your tracker and checklist are saved only on your device. There is no account and no login.
  • {{ icon('ban', 22) }}No ads, and we never sell or share student data.

Who pays, and why it stays free

Arhata is always free for students. It replaces middlemen who charge ₹500–2,000 per application. The cost is carried by organisations that already spend money to reach these students.
